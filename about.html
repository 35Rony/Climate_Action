Understanding Climate Change

The science, impacts, and solutions

Causes of Climate Change

Climate change is primarily caused by human activities that release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere. Understanding these causes is the first step toward meaningful action.

Rising Temperatures

Global average temperatures are increasing, causing heatwaves and changing weather patterns.

Melting Ice & Rising Seas

Polar ice caps and glaciers are melting, causing sea levels to rise and threatening coastal areas.

Extreme Weather

More frequent hurricanes, floods, droughts, and wildfires devastate communities worldwide.

Biodiversity Loss

Species extinction accelerates as habitats change faster than organisms can adapt.

Food & Water Insecurity

Changing rainfall patterns and temperatures affect agriculture and fresh water supplies.

Health Impacts

Heat stress, disease spread, and air pollution increasingly threaten human health.

Solutions to Climate Change

While the challenge is significant, we have the knowledge and technology to address climate change. Action at all levels is essential.

Renewable Energy

Transition to solar, wind, hydro, and other clean energy sources to replace fossil fuels.

Energy Efficiency

Improve building insulation, use LED lighting, and adopt efficient appliances.

Sustainable Transport

Use public transit, cycling, walking, and electric vehicles to reduce emissions.

Reforestation

Plant trees and restore forests to absorb CO2 and protect biodiversity.

Sustainable Agriculture

Adopt regenerative farming, reduce meat consumption, and minimize food waste.

Policy & Education

Support climate policies, educate communities, and drive systemic change.
